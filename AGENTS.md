## Brand colors

Defined in `src/static/styles/_variables.scss`. Always use these variables, never hardcode colors.

- Primary: `#5cd8d0` (hover `#acf4e6`), text on primary: `#0e0212`
- Accent: `#fad174`
- Secondary: `#8465cf`
- Background: `#120f14`
